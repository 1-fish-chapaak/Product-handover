  2. Percentages on the level-three company are the direct holding of its immediate parent; "effective" is the share reaching the listed parent through the chain. Altura Smart Metering Pvt Ltd is wholly owned by Altura Transmission Pvt Ltd, which is itself 74% owned — so 74% reaches the group.
