Amber, as this product defines it

There are two amber families and they are not interchangeable. high is the severity noun that sits in the middle of the RAG band; mitigated is the warning noun. Both are deliberately tilted away from stop-light amber — DESIGN.md calls for burnt orange, never #FFBF00. The ringed swatches are the ones the control page actually renders.

high — High severity

The amber of the RAG band. high-400 is what ragColor() returns for a score between 40 and 80; high-700 is the ink used for an amber number and its status word.
