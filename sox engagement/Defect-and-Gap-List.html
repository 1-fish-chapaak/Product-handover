D-22P2SOX · audit creation Materiality entered in the New audit wizard is not shown on the created audit. What worked correctly

Recording these matters as much as the defects.

  • The SOX severity engine is correct and auditable. "Show working" on DEF-A-01 printed all seven rules in order and the arithmetic held: the compensating control genuinely capped Material Weakness → Significant Deficiency, then aggregation across three exceptions sharing an assertion (₹36.40 Cr combined) raised it back to Material Weakness. Clearly-trivial also behaved — DEF-A-05 at ₹45.0 L fell under the ₹60.0 L floor and was logged, not evaluated.
  • The five-step control dossier works as designed — TOD concluding effective, population locked and versioned (POP-INT) with its source file and provenance recorded, a 25-item sample with its basis stated, per-attribute TOE evidence, and a sign-off step that names both preparer and reviewer.
  • Evidence gating is enforced. "Run AI validation" was disabled and relabelled "Upload the required file first", with an explicit warning.
  • All six confidence scores render live and correctly — RACM 0%, Control effectiveness 44%, Sample testing 1% at engagement level; Control completeness 100%, Evidence validated 0% (amber, gated), TOD coverage confidence 100% on the control.
  • The entity-instance model works — one control tested at four companies renders as four rows with independent due dates and statuses.
  • Plan Engagement creation — correct validation, successful save, accurate toast, immediate appearance on the timeline with the process count incrementing.
  • The New audit wizard computes materiality correctly and shows its effect before you commit. ₹420 Cr × 5% produced overall ₹21.00 Cr, PM ₹15.75 Cr (75%) and CTT ₹1.05 Cr (5%), and the "Where an exception would land" preview banded the ladder correctly — trivial ≤ ₹1.05 Cr, deficiency to ₹4.20 Cr, significant at ≥ ₹4.20 Cr (20% of overall), material weakness at ≥ ₹21.00 Cr.
  • Entity scoping is derived, not asked. Each company is scoped by comparing its balance against performance materiality, with the reason printed on the row ("₹648.50 Cr clears performance materiality" vs "₹8.00 Cr is below ₹15.75 Cr"), a group-coverage meter against a 60% target, and a warning when an in-scope parent's subsidiary is left out.
  • Roll-forward on a new round behaves correctly — all 45 controls reset to untested while the four open deficiencies carried forward.
  • The Create Engagement wizard is genuinely type-driven — choosing SOX / ICFR collapsed it from five steps to three, and "There are no separate entities" auto-filled the holding company and unblocked Continue.
  • Ask IRA execution — clarifying interview, five-step plan with dataset attribution, mid-run follow-up question, and a rendered result.
  • Role model is present throughout — Auditor / Risk Owner / Reviewer switching, "Your court" badges, a court column on the deficiency register, and "Nobody countersigns work they prepared" stated on the sign-off step.

Suggested fix order

  1. D-01 and D-02 together — almost certainly one root cause, and nothing else can be properly tested until navigation is reliable.
  2. D-22, D-04 and D-10 — three SOX correctness issues: a dashboard showing the wrong materiality, a population locked without a validated IPE, and testing proceeding on an unapproved RACM. These undermine the product's core claim, and D-22 is the sharpest of them.
  3. D-03 persistence — decide whether this build is meant to persist; if it is a demo, say so in the UI.
  4. D-05 breadcrumb — small fix, removes a dead end from the deepest screen.
  5. D-14 loading indicator — cheap, and takes the sting out of D-02 while the real fix lands.
  6. D-06 Ask IRA routing, then D-08 and D-15, then the rest.
